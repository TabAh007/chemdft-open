# ChemDFT Anti-Slop Checklist

Use this at design review and before every UI milestone. A failed item needs a named exception tied to task/scientific meaning—not taste.

## Reject

- [ ] A rounded rectangle or shadow around every section.
- [ ] Cards inside cards or card-shaped navigation.
- [ ] Giant pills, oversized active blocks, or pill tabs/sidebar rows.
- [ ] Decorative gradients, blobs, glassmorphism, neon sci-fi, or glowing AI icons.
- [ ] Oversized application headings, hero-like whitespace, or marketing copy inside the workstation.
- [ ] Identical three-column feature grids used as default landing-page rhythm.
- [ ] Decorative icons on every label, giant emojis, random categorical chart colors.
- [ ] Floating controls/panels without spatial or task justification.
- [ ] Shadows used on docked/flat hierarchy.
- [ ] Fake AI typing/activity, fake progress, fake metrics, or fake scientific values.
- [ ] Generic “Something went wrong” hiding parser, method, convergence, budget, or provider facts.
- [ ] Visualization altered to look attractive at the expense of geometry/data.
- [ ] AI explanation replacing raw data, settings, artifacts, or deterministic interactions.
- [ ] Compute credits as a sidebar marketing card or platform/AI/compute collapsed into one balance.
- [ ] Excessive badges that turn provenance/status into confetti.
- [ ] Visual complexity without a clear reading, selection, or action hierarchy.

## Require

- [ ] Central scientific work remains dominant at supported workstation widths.
- [ ] Hierarchy uses alignment, typography, indentation, hairlines, density, and purposeful whitespace.
- [ ] Active, selected, focused, hovered, running, warning, failed, approximate, and authoritative states are distinct.
- [ ] Controls are contextual/progressively disclosed; expert settings remain reachable.
- [ ] Units, precision, method/source, uncertainty, and provenance appear where decisions depend on them.
- [ ] Raw/source data and deterministic artifacts remain reachable.
- [ ] Every chart color/line/pattern and equation/formula has real meaning and context.
- [ ] AI plan, approval, cost, tool progress, evidence, and hypothesis are structured scientific blocks, not giant chat bubbles.
- [ ] Empty states offer direct domain actions without invented dashboard content.
- [ ] Error states state the exact failure and recovery choices.
- [ ] Layout works with long localized strings, zoom, keyboard, touch class, and reduced motion.
- [ ] Blueprint behavior is composed through ChemDFT tokens/primitives and does not look like a component demo.

## Landing-page proof

- [ ] First viewport shows the real mechanism, offer, and action—not only mood/claims.
- [ ] Interactive molecule/science content is deterministic or clearly labeled synthetic demonstration.
- [ ] No fake customer logos, testimonials, benchmarks, endpoints, capabilities, or pricing.
- [ ] Page cadence varies between demonstration, explanation, evidence, and quiet—not repeated feature cards.

## Impeccable review

Use Impeccable in implementation phases for bounded visual critique. The brief and this checklist remain binding: design tooling cannot override scientific integrity, accessibility, or confirmed direction.
